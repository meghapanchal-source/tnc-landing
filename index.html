PART ONE
Switch on digestion mode

There is a nerve running from your brain to your gut that decides how well digestion runs. If this goes off balance, food sits too long or moves too fast, and everything after that goes wrong — no matter how well you eat.

Nobody sells you anything for this, which is exactly why it gets skipped.

You'll leave knowing whether this is your problem, why the same meal behaves differently on holiday than on a Tuesday, and the two things I ask every client to do before they eat. They take under a minute and most people feel the difference inside a week.

PART TWO
Reset your microbiome

You are carrying a population in there, and when the balance shifts, food you have eaten your whole life starts behaving like a problem.

Cutting foods starves that population for a few weeks. That's your three good weeks. It never changes who is living there, which is why it always comes back and why the list keeps getting shorter.

You'll leave knowing whether yours needs rebuilding or clearing — they look identical from the outside and the approaches are close to opposite. Plus why simply taking a probiotic can be pointless.

PART THREE
Repair the gut lining

This is the part that explains the thing that frightens people most. Reacting to more and more foods, year after year, with no obvious reason.

Your gut lining is meant to be selective about what crosses into you. When it stops being selective, your immune system starts responding to food it handled perfectly well two years ago. That isn't twelve new intolerances. It's one problem, showing up twelve times.

You'll leave knowing how to repair it so that foods can come back. I'll show you what repair actually involves, roughly how long it takes, and the one mistake that keeps people cutting for years without ever fixing the reason they had to.

THE SYMPTOM ASSESSMENT BEGINS HERE

Recognise your own pattern

Same word - bloating. Completely different investigations.

“I wake up fine. By dinner my stomach is hard and swollen.”
What does that build tell me? What would point towards food, and what would point towards fermentation or how quickly things are moving?
“I feel full almost as soon as I start eating.”
Different pattern entirely. Now I want to know about appetite, reflux, nausea, medications, and what's happening higher up.
“I go every day, so I can't be constipated.”
You'd be surprised how often I hear this. We'll look at what normal bowel function actually means, and why frequency alone doesn't tell me enough.

What we are deliberately not

Ninety minutes is not long. So let me tell you what I've left out, and why.

  1. 1
    I'm not going to randomly tell you to cut out foods. Sometimes removing something is exactly right - but only once I know why. Guess wrong and you've given up a food group for nothing, and your list gets shorter again.
  2. 2
    I'm not going to hand you a probiotic to buy - even though probiotics do help some people enormously. The right one depends entirely on what's actually happening. The wrong one does nothing, and occasionally makes things worse. You've probably already proved that to yourself.
  3. 3
    I'm not going to send everyone off for testing - even though testing is a large part of my practice. A test tells you what's there. It doesn't tell you why. Order one without a clear question and you get a colourful report, a fright, and a shopping list. Some of you will need one. Some of you absolutely will not, and I'd rather you kept the money.
  4. 4
    And I can't diagnose you in a room full of people. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something. What I can do is show you how to work out which direction actually makes sense for you - which is the step everybody skips, and the reason the last few years haven't worked.
